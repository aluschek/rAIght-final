# ADR 0008 – Independent vendor framing

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Supersedes:** parts of [ADR 0005](0005-scope-external-agents.md), decisions 3 and 4 (the earlier business framing)

## Context

Since ADR 0005, the pitch framed us as an internal taskforce of one large company, with rAIght first used internally and sold later. Framing rAIght as a product for any company with the same problem is simpler to explain and gives the business case real customers and real revenue.

## Decision

1. **We are an independent company** (vendor) that builds and sells rAIght.
2. **Customers are companies** whose employees build AI automations with external tools. The **buyer is the customer's CIO office**; the **users are its process owners** (persona Lena, Recruiting Operations Lead in HR at a customer company). The sustainability office stays a stakeholder.
3. **Value proposition unchanged:** cut token spending and CO₂ emissions of employee-built agents and automations without slowing adoption – *Not more AI, but rAIght.*
4. **No framing around one specific company** in concept, demo, UI or pitch. The customer's own net-zero and reporting goals are the motivation.
5. Scope (ADR 0005 decisions 1, 2 and 5) and the pure clickable demo (ADR 0006) are unchanged.

## Consequences

- Concept docs 01, 02, 04, 05, 06, 08, 09, the demo script, design system and README are reworded; no feature or data change.
- The business model now has **real revenue streams**; the revenue model and business case are reworked (concept 04 keeps a placeholder).
- The "taskforce" glossary term is replaced by *Customer* and *rAIght (vendor)*.
